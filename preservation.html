Shared memory capsule

Paid Preservation Policy

Last updated: July 27, 2026

You pay once when sealing an eligible Shared Capsoul. The quote covers its waiting period until the scheduled opening date; recipients do not pay to receive or open it.

1. Preservation units

A Preservation Month and a Preservation Year are consumable App Store products. Each purchase adds one non-expiring unit to the matching balance. Units do not represent a subscription, recurring renewal, stored cash value, or a right to withdraw money.

Only the sender pays. The same quote applies regardless of recipient count: sending one shared capsule to one person or to five recipients consumes the same time-based units.

2. How a quote is calculated

Capsoul measures started UTC periods from the sending instant to the scheduled opening instant. Any started month or year counts as a complete unit. A capsule lasting no more than one month uses one Preservation Month; three months and 17 days uses four Preservation Months.

For longer intervals, the app may quote mixed Preservation Months and Years when that uses the available unit types appropriately. For example, one year and three months may consume one Preservation Year plus three Preservation Months. A Preservation Year covers an interval of up to one year and is priced more favorably than twelve separately purchased Preservation Months.

The server returns the final quote before sealing. The app shows the required combination and current balance, and sealing proceeds only after the sender confirms and has sufficient units.

3. Shared-capsule limits

A paid Shared capsule may include up to 500 MB of content and up to five recipients. Recipient-count independence applies within that limit. Personal capsules are not charged under this Shared paid-preservation model.

4. Opening and cloud release

At the scheduled time, recipients are notified that the capsule can be opened. In a multi-recipient capsule, participants may receive opening-progress updates. After all required recipients open, Capsoul begins a 24-hour release period before eligible cloud media is deleted.

Opened memories become local documents on the participating devices. After cloud deletion, they may be local-only and carry recovery risk: deleting the app or its data, losing or replacing a device, signing out before a completed local save, or losing a backup may permanently lose the local document. Capsoul cannot guarantee server recovery after release.

5. Withdrawal and expiration

The sender may withdraw an eligible capsule before any recipient opens it. Withdrawal does not return consumed preservation units. The units paid for the reserved waiting period are not restored, including when a capsule is withdrawn shortly after sending.

If an unregistered recipient does not claim the capsule within the 90-day claim window after its scheduled opening, their claim may expire under the service rules. This does not return consumed units.

6. App Store refunds and balance debt

Apple decides App Store refund requests. When Apple reports a refunded consumable transaction, Capsoul reverses the units originally credited from that transaction. If they were already spent, the account receives a same-kind balance debt: refunded Preservation Months reduce only the month balance, and refunded Preservation Years reduce only the year balance. Future units of that kind satisfy the debt before becoming available.

7. Existing users and grants

Capsules created under the legacy policy remain permanently free; migration does not make existing users pay to keep or open those capsules. This treatment attaches to eligible legacy capsules and is not a temporary subscription benefit.

Eligible Day 1 users receive a one-time grant of 60 Preservation Months and ten Preservation Years. The owner reviews eligibility before the grant is run. A promotional Offer Code is redeemed through Apple and follows Apple’s redemption rules; an administrative grant is a separate balance adjustment made by authorized Capsoul operations. Neither changes the treatment of legacy-free capsules.

8. Records, changes, and help

We maintain transaction and balance-ledger records needed to prevent duplicate credit, reconcile Apple notifications, explain consumption, handle refunds, and investigate disputes. Material policy changes will be communicated as appropriate and do not retroactively convert eligible legacy-free capsules into paid ones.

For quote or balance questions, contact support@capsoul.chat and include the account email and capsule or App Store transaction reference when available. Do not send passwords, verification codes, or Apple credentials.
