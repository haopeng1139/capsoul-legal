Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 22, 2026

Capsoul ("we", "our", or "us") is a time capsule app that lets you seal memories and unlock them in the future. This policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, and how we protect it.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information — your email address and display name, collected when you create an account.
  • Capsule content — text, photos, videos, voice recordings, and files you choose to attach to a capsule. This content is stored securely in Firebase Storage and Firestore.
  • Recipient email addresses — email addresses you enter when creating a shared capsule.
  • Location name — an optional text label you add to a capsule. We do not continuously track your location.
  • Device information — basic crash and error data collected automatically by Firebase to help us fix bugs.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate you and secure your account.
  • To store your capsules and deliver them to recipients when the unlock date arrives.
  • To enable Face ID / biometric sign-in on your device (credentials never leave your device).
  • To improve app stability using crash reports.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with any third party for marketing purposes.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on Google Firebase infrastructure (Firebase Auth, Firestore, and Firebase Storage) protected by Google's security standards. Access to capsule data is restricted so that only the sender and designated recipients can view a capsule's contents.

4. Data Retention

Your account and all associated capsules remain stored until you delete your account. You can request account deletion at any time from within the app (Profile → Delete Account) or by emailing us at the address below. We will delete your data within 30 days of a verified request.

5. Children's Privacy

Capsoul is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

6. Third-Party Services

We use Google Firebase, which has its own privacy policy. No other third-party analytics or advertising SDKs are included in the app.

7. Your Rights

You may access, correct, or delete your personal data at any time. To exercise these rights, use the in-app account settings or contact us at the email address below.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through the app or by email. Continued use of Capsoul after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
